# Task35
Veda Technology Business & Service Analytics

Project Description

This project focuses on building a complete Data Science Analytics solution for Veda Technology using simulated business data.

The project analyzes different business areas, including:

- Website Visitors
- Internship Applications
- Training Programs
- IT Services
- Digital Services
- Customer Inquiries
- Customer and Service Requests
- Marketing Channels
- Business Performance

Objectives

- Analyze business data using Python.
- Understand customer behavior and business performance.
- Perform customer and service analytics.
- Analyze marketing channels.
- Identify useful business insights.
- Apply forecasting techniques.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Excel
- Power BI
- SQL
- Git and GitHub

Project Workflow

1. Collect simulated business data.
2. Clean and preprocess the data.
3. Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
4. Analyze customer and business performance.
5. Create visualizations and dashboards.
6. Apply forecasting and analytical techniques.
7. Generate insights to support business decisions.

Expected Outcomes

- Better understanding of business performance.
- Identification of customer trends.
- Analysis of service requests and inquiries.
- Understanding of marketing effectiveness.
- Data-driven business recommendations.

Conclusion

This project provides practical exposure to Data Science and Business Analytics by applying Python, data visualization, statistical analysis, and business intelligence techniques to simulated business data.
